Monitored vs. self-monitored: which do you actually need?

If your main goal is checking in on a package or pet, the self-monitored cameras and locks compared elsewhere on this site are enough — you get an alert, you look at your phone. Monitored security earns its monthly fee when it matters most: break-ins, fire, and medical emergencies, situations where you might not see the alert in time. If that's the risk you're covering, the monthly cost is the price of someone else always watching.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need professional monitoring, or is a self-monitored camera enough?

Self-monitored cameras send alerts to your phone, but nobody dispatches help if you don't see it. Professional monitoring adds a 24/7 team that calls you and contacts police or fire services automatically.

What does monitored home security typically cost?

Expect separate equipment and monthly monitoring costs — monitoring commonly runs from about $20 to $60 per month depending on the provider and plan tier.

Are long contracts required?

It depends on the provider — some offer month-to-month monitoring, others require a 1–3 year commitment, often in exchange for discounted equipment. Confirm current terms directly with the provider.

Once you've picked a plan, check whether it also lowers your home insurance premium — most monitored systems qualify for a discount.
